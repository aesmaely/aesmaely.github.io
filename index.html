Focus Areas

Research Interests

Transportation Equity

Examining barriers that limit mobility for vulnerable and underserved populations across urban and rural contexts.

Transportation Policy Analysis

Evaluating how national and regional planning policies translate—or fail to translate—into local transportation outcomes.

Transportation & Public Health

Investigating the intersection of mobility limitations and healthcare access, including telehealth and vision care contexts.

Spatial Analysis & GIS

Using GIS tools to map infrastructure, delineate boundaries, and reveal spatial patterns in community planning challenges.

AI in Transportation

Exploring machine learning and AI-assisted qualitative methods to advance transportation research and planning practice.

Informal Settlements

Drawing on field experience in slum upgrading to inform equity-centered planning in communities with limited formal infrastructure.
